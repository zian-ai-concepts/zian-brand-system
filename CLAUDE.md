# CLAUDE.md

This repository contains the ZIAN AI Concepts corporate design system.

When creating, editing or reviewing files in this repository, follow the ZIAN brand system.

## Brand Direction

ZIAN is:
- monolithic
- precise
- technical
- modular
- dark
- strategic
- calm
- premium

Avoid:
- playful colors
- generic startup gradients
- loud marketing language
- inconsistent layout
- duplicated brand values
- distorted logo usage

## Colors

Use these tokens:

- Void Black: #000000
- Obsidian: #050505
- Deep Graphite: #0A0A0A
- Carbon Grey: #222222
- Iron Grey: #2F2F2F
- Steel Grey: #555555
- Titanium: #888888
- Silver Mist: #CCCCCC
- Monolith White: #F6F6EE
- Aura Silver: #E8EEE2
- Soft Alloy: #DDE4DA

## Typography

Preferred:
- Display: Space Grotesk or Sora
- Body: Inter or IBM Plex Sans
- Mono: JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono

## Logo

Use original logo assets only.
Do not recreate or approximate the logo.
Do not distort the logo.
Keep sufficient clear space.
Place the logo optically centered in circular or aura-based compositions.

## Content Voice

Use a clear, technical and strategic tone.
Prefer precise wording over promotional language.
